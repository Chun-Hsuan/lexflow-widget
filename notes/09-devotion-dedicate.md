---
title: devotion vs dedicate
type: 辨析
core: devote 與 dedicate 多半可互換；dedicate 另有題獻之意；to 是介系詞，接動作用 V-ing。
---

## 結論

- devotion / devote 和 dedication / dedicate 都翻成「奉獻」，在多數句子中可以互換。
- 兩者後面的 to 都是介系詞，要接動作時一定用 V-ing。
- dedicate 有 devote 沒有的用法：把書、歌曲「題獻」給某人，以及 dedicated「專用的」。

## 可以混用

下面兩句意思相同，換字都通順：

- He devoted his life to science. / He dedicated his life to science.（他把一生奉獻給科學。）
- 注意 devote 的結構是 devote A to B；dedicate 的結構是 dedicate A to B，兩者完全相同。

常見搭配的傾向（不是硬規則）：

- devoted 常用來形容出於愛的人：devoted parents、devoted fan、devoted husband。
- dedicated 常用來形容投入工作或事業的人：a dedicated teacher、a dedicated team。

## 用法不同

**1. dedicate 可以表示「題獻」，devote 不行**

- 對：The author dedicated the book to his parents.*（作者把這本書題獻給他的父母。）
- 錯：The author devoted the book to his parents.

**2. dedicated 可以表示「專用的」**

- a dedicated phone line（專用電話線）*

**3. to 後面接動作要用 V-ing**

- 對：She devoted herself to helping others. / She dedicated herself to helping others.*
- 錯：She devoted herself to help others.

## 核心對照

| 單字 | 詞性 | 含意 | 常見搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|
| devotion | 名詞 | 出於愛、熱情或虔誠的全心投入與忠誠 | devotion to + N/V-ing；show devotion |
| dedication | 名詞 | 專心致志，長期為某目標努力 | require / take dedication |
| devote | 及物動詞 | 把……奉獻給；將……專用於 | devote A to B；devote oneself to |
| dedicate | 及物動詞 | 奉獻；致力於；題獻 | dedicate A to B；dedicate oneself to |
| devoted | 形容詞 | 全心投入的；忠實的 | be devoted to |
| dedicated | 形容詞 | 盡職盡責的；專用的 | a dedicated teacher |

## 例句

- His devotion to his family is truly admirable.（他對家庭的奉獻令人欽佩。）
- She devotes her weekends to volunteering.（她把週末奉獻給志願服務。）
- They are devoted parents who always put their kids first.（他們是全心全意付出的父母，總是把孩子放在第一位。）
- It takes years of dedication to become a professional pianist.（要成為職業鋼琴家需要多年的專心奉獻。）
- The scientist dedicated his entire life to finding a cure.（這位科學家將他的一生奉獻於尋找治療方法。）
- She is a dedicated teacher who loves her students.（她是一位盡職奉獻的老師，非常愛她的學生。）

## 翻譯與作文

- 中文對應：「把時間／精力用在……」→ devote A to B；「致力於」→ dedicate oneself to；「題獻」→ dedicate。
- 作文句型：___ devoted much time to ___.
  - He devoted much time to practicing the piano.*
