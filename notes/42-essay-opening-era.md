type: 句型

## 結論

- 時代背景開頭先背一個最安全的說法，需要變化時再換第二個。
- 容易丟分的地方：字形寫錯（modern time、technology era）、era of 和 era when 的後面搞混、Nowadays 搭配過去式。

## 最安全的五個

1. Nowadays, ___.
2. In recent years, ___ has / have ___.
3. In today's world, ___.
4. In an era when ___, ___.
5. With the rise of ___, ___.

## 句型與示範

| 想表達 | 句型 | 示範句 |
|---|---|---|
| 現在這個時代 | In the digital age, ___ | In the digital age, information spreads faster than ever before. |
| 時代特色 | In an era when ___, ___ | In an era when everyone owns a smartphone, face-to-face conversation has become rare. |
| 現況 | Nowadays, ___ | Nowadays, more and more teenagers spend hours on social media every day. |
| 長期趨勢 | In recent years, ___ | In recent years, online shopping has become part of our daily lives. |
| 大環境 | In today's world, ___ | In today's world, thinking critically is as important as having knowledge. |
| 近期事件 | Recently, ___ | Recently, a new study has shown that sleep affects memory. |
| 強調現代 | In this day and age, ___ | In this day and age, it is hard to imagine life without the Internet. |
| 某事物興起 | With the rise of ___, ___ | With the rise of artificial intelligence, many jobs are changing. |

## 容易錯的地方

- era of + 名詞，era when + 子句。
  - 錯：in the era of everyone owns a smartphone
  - 對：in the era of smartphones / in an era when everyone owns a smartphone
- era 和 age 可以混用：in the era of AI / in the age of AI。
- 錯：in modern time → 對：in modern times
- 不自然：in this technology era → 較自然：in this technological era / in the digital age
- 錯：in recently years → 對：in recent years
- 錯：in nowadays world → 對：in today's world
- nowadays 只談現在，不能搭過去式。
  - 錯：Nowadays, he went to school by bike.
- in recent years 談長期趨勢，搭現在完成式；recently 談短時間內的事。
- 中文的「近日」，作文裡通常想說的是「近年來」，用 in recent years 比較準確。

## 翻譯示範

- 在這個科技時代，人們很難離開手機。→ In this technological era, it is hard for people to live without their phones.
- 近年來，越來越多學生選擇線上學習。→ In recent years, more and more students have chosen online learning.

## 升級替換

- Nowadays 不要每段都用，可輪流換成 in recent years / in today's world。
- 想讓開頭更有層次：In an era when ___，但後面一定要接完整的子句。
