## 結論

- 運動主題的作文可以分成三塊：論述段（好處與壞處）、個人經驗段（形成習慣）、結尾段（呼籲與建議）。
- exercise 當「運動」是不可數名詞；「一次健身」是 a workout；動詞 work out 是兩個字，名詞 workout 才連在一起。

## 第二段：論述運動的好處與缺乏運動的壞處

**好處**

- keep fit / stay in shape（保持體態）
- strengthen our bodies / build up our strength（增強體能）
- boost our immune system（增強免疫力）
- relieve stress / reduce stress（減輕壓力）
- improve our mood（改善心情）
- sleep better（睡得更好）
- lose weight / burn calories（減重、燃燒熱量）

**壞處**

- a lack of exercise（缺乏運動）
- gain weight（變胖）
- suffer from health problems（受健康問題所苦）
- be at risk of ___（有___的風險）：be at risk of heart disease
- feel tired all the time（一直覺得疲倦）

**論述句型**

- **句型 1**：Regular exercise not only ___ but also ___.
  - Regular exercise not only keeps us fit but also helps us relieve stress.（規律運動不僅讓我們保持體態，還能幫助我們減輕壓力。）
- **句型 2**：A lack of exercise can lead to ___.
  - A lack of exercise can lead to weight gain and a number of health problems.（缺乏運動可能導致體重增加和許多健康問題。）
- **句型 3**：It is important for ___ to ___.
  - It is important for students to get enough exercise, especially during exam season.（學生要有足夠的運動量很重要，尤其是在考試期間。）

## 個人經驗段

**形成習慣**

- make it a habit to V（養成做某事的習慣）
- develop a habit of V-ing（養成___的習慣）
- stick to ___ / keep up ___（堅持、持續）
- take up a sport（開始從事一項運動）
- work out（健身；動詞是兩個字）

**經驗句型**

- **句型 1**：I make it a habit to ___ every ___.
  - I make it a habit to go jogging every morning before school.（我養成了每天早上上學前慢跑的習慣。）
- **句型 2**：Since I started ___, I have felt ___.
  - Since I started working out three times a week, I have felt much more energetic.（自從我開始每週健身三次，我覺得精力充沛多了。）
- **句型 3**：It was not easy to stick to ___, but ___.
  - It was not easy to stick to my exercise plan, but the results were worth the effort.（要堅持運動計畫並不容易，但成果值得這份努力。）

## 結尾段

- Only by ___ can we ___.（只有……，我們才能___）
  - Only by exercising regularly can we stay healthy in the long run.（只有規律運動，我們才能長期保持健康。）
  - Only by 放句首時，後面要倒裝，變成 can we。*
- 建議在兩者之間取得平衡：keep / strike a balance between ___ and ___（強調平衡）
  - We should keep a balance between study and exercise.（我們應該在讀書與運動之間取得平衡。）

## 字詞對應

- 「運動」→ exercise（不可數）：get regular exercise
- 「一次健身」→ a workout：do a thirty-minute workout
- 「保持健康」→ stay healthy / keep fit / stay in shape
- 「規律」→ regular exercise / exercise regularly / on a regular basis

## 中式英文陷阱（作文情境）

- 錯：Lack of exercise makes us become unhealth. → 對：A lack of exercise makes us unhealthy.
- 錯：Doing exercise is helpful to our health body. → 對：Exercising regularly is good for our health.
- 錯：keep our health → 對：stay healthy / keep fit
- 錯：I workout every day. → 對：I work out every day.
- 錯：sit all day without move → 對：sit all day without moving（without 後面接 V-ing）
