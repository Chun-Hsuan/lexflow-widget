## 結論

**draw** (v.)：畫；拉；吸引；得出；抽取。三態：draw - drew - drawn（不是 drawed）。名詞 draw 有「平手」的意思。

## 學測常見義項與句型

| 義項 | 核心句型 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 畫（用筆畫線條、圖） | draw sth / draw sb sth / draw sth for sb | She drew a picture of her family.（她畫了一幅家人的畫。） |
| 拉（窗簾）、拖、拉動 | draw the curtains；be drawn by | Please draw the curtains.（請把窗簾拉上。） |
| 吸引 | draw + attention / a crowd / tourists；be drawn to sth | The festival draws thousands of tourists every year.（這個慶典每年吸引數千名遊客。） |
| 得出、獲得 | draw a conclusion / a lesson / inspiration from | We can draw a conclusion from the data.（我們可以從資料中得出結論。） |
| 抽取、提領 | draw lots；draw money from the bank | They drew lots to decide who would go first.（他們抽籤決定誰先去。） |
| 接近（時間） | draw near / close；draw to an end | The exam is drawing near.（考試快到了。） |
| 平手 | end in a draw；the two teams drew | The match ended in a draw.（比賽以平手收場。） |

## 常見搭配

- draw a picture / a line：畫圖、畫線
- draw (sb's) attention to sth：引起（某人）對某事的注意
- draw a conclusion (from sth)：（從某事）得出結論
- draw a lesson from sth：從某事得到教訓
- draw a crowd：吸引人潮
- be drawn to sth / sb：被……吸引。I was drawn to the music.（我被這首音樂吸引。）

## 相關片語（draw + 副詞或介系詞）

- draw up：擬定、起草。The lawyer drew up a contract.（律師擬定了一份合約。）
- draw on：利用、借助（經驗、知識）。She drew on her experience to write the story.（她運用自己的經驗寫出這個故事。）
- draw out：拉長、引出。The teacher drew out the shy student by asking easy questions.（老師用簡單的問題引導害羞的學生開口。）
- draw back：退縮、退後。

## 搭配不同

**得出結論用 draw，不是 make**

- 不自然：We can make a conclusion from the survey.
- 對：We can draw a conclusion from the survey.（我們可以從這份調查得出結論。）
- reach / come to a conclusion 也通順：We reached the conclusion that ...*

**引起注意：draw attention to，介系詞用 to**

- 錯：The poster draws people's attention for the problem.
- 對：The poster draws people's attention to the problem.（這張海報讓大家注意到這個問題。）

## 使用範圍不同

**draw 是用筆畫線條，用顏料畫是 paint**

- 對：He drew a map with a pencil.（他用鉛筆畫了一張地圖。）
- 對：She painted the wall blue.（她把牆漆成藍色。）
- 錯：She drew the wall blue.

## 可以混用

- draw / attract 在「吸引」的意思上通常可以互換：The museum draws / attracts many visitors.
- draw 後面常接 attention、crowd、tourist，attract 也一樣通順，不用硬分。

## 常見錯誤

- 錯：He drawed a picture yesterday. → 對：He drew a picture yesterday.
- 錯：I have drew a line. → 對：I have drawn a line.
- 錯：I was drawing to the music. → 對：I was drawn to the music.（被吸引用被動式 be drawn to）

## 詞性變化與同字根字

- drawing (n.) 圖畫、繪畫
- drawer (n.) 抽屜
- drawback (n.) 缺點、不利之處：The plan has one major drawback.（這個計畫有一個主要缺點。）
- withdraw (v.) 提領、撤回、退出：withdraw money from the bank（從銀行提款）

## 翻譯與作文

**中文對應**

- 「畫圖、畫線」→ draw
- 「得出結論」→ draw a conclusion
- 「吸引注意、吸引遊客」→ draw attention / tourists
- 「引起某人注意到……」→ draw sb's attention to
- 「擬定計畫、合約」→ draw up a plan / contract
- 提醒：「做出決定」是 make a decision，「得出結論」才是 draw a conclusion，兩個動詞不能互換。

**翻譯示範**

- 中文：從這份調查中，我們可以得出一個結論：多數學生睡眠不足。
  英文：From this survey, we can draw the conclusion that most students don't get enough sleep.
  - 「從這份調查中」→ From this survey；「得出一個結論」→ draw the conclusion；「結論：多數學生……」→ that 子句，補充說明 conclusion 的內容。容易翻錯在用 make a conclusion。
- 中文：這座古老的小鎮每年吸引許多遊客。
  英文：The ancient town draws many tourists every year.
  - 「吸引」直接用 draw 當及物動詞，後面接受詞 many tourists，不需要加 to。

**作文用法**

適合用在議論說理（舉證後下結論、說明某件事引起關注）和心得反思（從經驗中得到教訓）兩類段落。

- 句型一：From ___, we can draw the conclusion that ___.
  - From these examples, we can draw the conclusion that hard work leads to success.（從這些例子，我們可以得出結論：努力會帶來成功。）
- 句型二：___ draw(s) public attention to ___.
  - The documentary drew public attention to the problem of plastic waste.（這部紀錄片讓大眾注意到塑膠垃圾的問題。）
- 句型三：I drew a lesson from ___.
  - I drew a lesson from my failure: preparation is always important.（我從失敗中學到一個教訓：準備永遠很重要。）

**升級替換**

- get a conclusion → draw a conclusion
- make people notice → draw people's attention to
- 不要硬用：口語敘述「畫圖」時 draw a picture 就是最自然的說法，不需要換成別的字。

**中式英文陷阱**

- 不自然：We make a conclusion that students need more sleep.
- 對：We draw the conclusion that students need more sleep.
- 錯：The news drew many people's attention for the issue.
- 對：The news drew many people's attention to the issue.
