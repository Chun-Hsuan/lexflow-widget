---
title: capacity vs capability
type: 辨析
core: 「能力」義可混用；「容量」只能用 capacity；capacity for N、capability of V-ing。
---

## 結論

capacity 和 capability 只有在「能力」這個義項上可以混用。「容量、容納量」只能用 capacity，「做事的能力範圍」多半用 capability。

## 不能混用的地方

**使用範圍不同：容量、容納量只能用 capacity**

- 對：The stadium has a seating capacity of 50,000.（這座體育館有五萬個座位。）
- 對：The hall was filled to capacity.（大廳座無虛席。）
- 錯：The stadium has a seating capability of 50,000.
- 錯：The hall was filled to capability.
- 錯：The bottle has a capability of one liter.
- 對：The bottle has a capacity of one liter.（這個瓶子的容量是一公升。）

**搭配不同：for N 的能力用 capacity，of V-ing 的能力用 capability**

- 對：Human beings have a great capacity for love.（人類有很大的愛的能力。）
- 錯：Human beings have a great capability for love.
- 對：The new system has the capability of processing large amounts of data.（新系統有處理大量資料的能力。）
- 錯：The new system has the capacity of processing large amounts of data.
- 後面接 to V 時，兩個字都可以：capacity to V、capability to V。

## 可以混用的地方

「有能力做某事」這個義項，兩字可以直接換：

- He has the capacity to lead the team. = He has the capability to lead the team.（他有能力帶領這個團隊。）

## 搭配整理

**capacity**

- seating capacity（座位數）、storage capacity（儲存容量）、production capacity（產能）
- at full capacity（滿載運作）、be filled to capacity（客滿）
- the capacity to V / for N（……的能力）
- increase / expand capacity（增加、擴大容量或產能）

**capability**

- have the capability to V / of V-ing（有能力做……）
- military / technological capabilities（軍事、技術能力，常用複數）
- beyond one's capabilities（超出某人的能力）
- improve one's capabilities（提升能力）

## 相關字

- capable（adj.）：be capable of + V-ing（有能力做……），後面一定接 of。
  - She is capable of handling the problem by herself.（她有能力自己處理這個問題。）
- incapable（adj.）：be incapable of + V-ing（沒有能力做……）。
- ability：最常見、最安全的「能力」，the ability to V。寫作時不確定就用它。

## 翻譯與作文（選字指南）

**中文對應**

- 容量、容納人數、座位數、產能、客滿 → capacity
- 愛、同情、學習的「能力、潛力」→ capacity for ___
- 做事、解決問題的能力 → ability 最安全；capacity / capability 也可以
- 機構、技術、軍事的「能力」→ capability（常用複數）
- 提醒：「能力很強」的人，用形容詞 capable 最自然，不用寫 His capability is strong。

**翻譯示範 1**
這間教室的容納人數是四十人。
→ The classroom has a capacity of forty people. / The classroom can hold forty people.

- 「容納人數」對應 capacity，用 a capacity of + 數字。
- 這裡不能用 capability，因為講的是空間大小。

**翻譯示範 2**
年輕人有能力改變世界。
→ Young people have the capability to change the world.

- 「有能力做……」對應 have the capability to V，也可以換成 capacity 或 ability。
- 「改變世界」用 change the world，不用加 to。

**作文句型**

- Everyone has the capacity to ___ if ___.（議論說理、心得反思）
  - Everyone has the capacity to grow if they are willing to learn from mistakes.
- ___ has the capability of ___ing.（說明科技、工具的功能）
  - This app has the capability of translating speech into text in real time.
- The ___ was filled to capacity.（經驗敘述、描述場面）
  - The auditorium was filled to capacity when the singer appeared on stage.

**升級替換**

- can hold 50,000 people → has a capacity of 50,000
- be good at solving problems → be capable of solving problems
- 日常小事（I can swim.）不要硬換，能力的基本用法用 ability 或 be able to 就夠。

**中式英文陷阱**

- 錯：The room has a capability of twenty people. → 對：The room has a capacity of twenty people.
- 錯：He has the capacity of solving difficult problems. → 對：He has the capability of solving difficult problems. / He has the capacity to solve difficult problems.
- 錯：The theater was full of capability. → 對：The theater was filled to capacity.
