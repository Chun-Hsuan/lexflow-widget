---
title: 表示「因為」的字與片語
type: 辨析
core: 接子句用 because，接名詞用 because of；thanks to 只用在好的結果。
---

## 結論

- 「因為」後面接子句，預設用 because；後面接名詞，預設用 because of。
- as、since 可以在句首替換 because；for 學測作文少用。
- owing to、on account of、as a result of、due to 都接名詞，大多可以和 because of 互換，差別只在正式程度。
- thanks to 專門用在好的結果，不能當成一般的「因為」。

## 一、接子句：because / as / since / for

| | because | as / since | for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 位置 | 句首、句中都可以 | 多放句首 | 只能放句中，前面通常有逗號 |
| 能否回答 Why 問句 | 可以 | 不行 | 不行 |
| 學測作文 | 最常用 | 可用 | 少用 |

### 用法不同

**回答 Why 問句，只能用 because**

- 對：Why were you late? --- Because the bus broke down.（你為什麼遲到？因為公車拋錨了。）
- 錯：Why were you late? --- As the bus broke down.

**強調原因的 It is ... that ... 句型，只能用 because**

- 對：It is because he is new that we should be patient with him.（正是因為他是新人，我們才應該對他有耐心。）
- 錯：It is as he is new that we should be patient with him.

**for 不能放句首**

- 對：I went to bed early, for I was tired.（我早早上床睡覺，因為我累了。）
- 錯：For I was tired, I went to bed early.
- for 當連接詞時前面要有逗號，語氣偏書面，作文不如 because 自然。
- 注意：for him 這種寫法是介系詞（為了他），不是「因為」。

**as 可能有歧義**

- As he was walking home, he saw an accident.（當他走路回家時，看到一場意外。這裡是「當時」。）
- As he was new, we made allowances for him.（因為他是新人，我們體諒他。這裡是「因為」。）
- 想確定表示原因時，用 because。

### 可以混用

- 句首的「因為……，所以……」，because / as / since 都通順：
  - Because / As / Since it was raining, we stayed at home.（因為下雨，我們待在家裡。）
- 原因是新資訊用 because、原因是大家都知道用 as / since，只是常見傾向，不是規則，不需要特別背。

## 二、接名詞：because of / owing to / on account of / as a result of / due to / thanks to

這些後面接名詞或 V-ing，不能直接接完整子句。

| 說法 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| because of | 因為 | He was absent because of his illness.（他因為生病缺席。） |
| owing to | 由於（書面） | Owing to the heavy rain, the match was cancelled.（由於大雨，比賽取消了。） |
| on account of | 由於（書面，較少見） | The flight was delayed on account of the fog.（班機因為起霧而延誤。） |
| as a result of | 因……的結果 | As a result of the typhoon, all classes were suspended.（因為颱風，全部停課。） |
| due to | 由於、起因於 | The delay was due to the heavy traffic.（延誤是因為交通壅塞。） |
| thanks to | 多虧（通常是好的結果） | Thanks to your help, I passed the exam.（多虧你的幫忙，我通過了考試。） |

### 可以混用

- because of、owing to、on account of、as a result of、due to 放進同一個句子，意思通常都通順，差別只在正式程度。
- 寫作時 because of 最穩；想換口氣可以用 owing to 或 due to；on account of 認得即可。

### 用法不同

**接子句時要加 the fact that**

- 錯：Owing to he was ill, he was absent.
- 對：Owing to his illness, he was absent.
- 對：Owing to the fact that he was ill, he was absent.
- on account of、due to、as a result of 同樣適用。

**because of 後面不能接完整子句**

- 錯：He was absent because of he was ill.
- 對：He was absent because he was ill. / He was absent because of his illness.

**as a result 和 as a result of 不一樣**

- as a result（沒有 of）表示「結果」，後面接逗號和完整句子：
  - He didn't study. As a result, he failed the test.（他沒有念書，結果考試不及格。）
- as a result of 表示「因為」，後面接名詞或 V-ing：
  - He failed the test as a result of not studying.

**thanks to 通常用在好的結果**

- 對：Thanks to your advice, I finished on time.（多虧你的建議，我準時完成了。）
- 抱怨時改用 because of：I was late because of the traffic.

## 翻譯與作文

### 選字指南

| 情境 | 用哪個 |
|---|---|
| 回答 Why、強調原因、不確定怎麼選 | because |
| 句首「既然、由於」 | as / since |
| 想寫得書面、補充說明理由 | for（少用） |
| 後面接名詞或 V-ing | because of（想換口氣用 owing to / due to） |
| 多虧某人、某事帶來好結果 | thanks to |

### 翻譯示範

1. 中文：因為他才剛進公司，所以我們應該體諒他。
   英文：Because he has just joined the company, we should be understanding of him.
   - 「因為……所以……」英文只留 because，主要子句不加 so。
   - 「進公司」是 join the company，不是 get in the company；「剛……」用現在完成式 has just joined。
2. 中文：由於大雨，比賽取消了。
   英文：Owing to the heavy rain, the match was cancelled.
   - 「大雨」是名詞片語，所以用 owing to 或 because of，不用 because。

### 作文句型

- ___ is important because ___.
  - Reading is important because it broadens our horizons.（閱讀很重要，因為它拓展我們的視野。）
- It is because ___ that ___.
  - It is because we are willing to work together that we can finish the project on time.
- Owing to / Due to ___, ___.
  - Due to the rapid development of technology, our lives have become much more convenient.

### 升級替換

- 避免一直重複 because：句首可交替用 as / since；後面接名詞時可交替用 owing to / due to / because of。但不要每句都換，自然最重要。

### 中式英文陷阱

- 錯：Because he was ill, so he was absent. → 對：Because he was ill, he was absent. / He was ill, so he was absent.
- 錯：He was absent. Because he was ill. → 對：He was absent because he was ill.
- 錯：Because of he was ill, ... → 對：Because he was ill, ... / Because of his illness, ...
