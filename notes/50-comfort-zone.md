---
title: comfort zone 與 status quo
type: 辨析
core: 談個人挑戰用 step out of one's comfort zone；the status quo 是「現狀」，固定加 the。
---

## 結論

講個人成長、挑戰自己，最自然的說法是 **step out of one's comfort zone**，不是 the status quo。status quo 也能用，但角度不同：

- **comfort zone**：說的是「讓自己感到安心、熟悉的範圍」，重點在個人的心理狀態。
- **the status quo**：說的是「目前既有的狀態」，重點在維持或改變，範圍可以是個人、學校或社會。

## 使用範圍不同

| | comfort zone | the status quo |
|---|---|---|
| 對應中文 | 舒適圈 | 現狀 |
| 常用主題 | 個人嘗試新事物、克服恐懼 | 制度、社會、個人處境，都可以 |
| 冠詞 | 可數名詞，a / my / our 都可以 | 固定 the，不能換成 my |
| 常搭動詞 | step out of、leave、stay in | maintain、challenge、accept |

兩者可以並用，意思互相呼應：

- Staying in our comfort zone means accepting the status quo.（待在舒適圈，就等於接受現狀。）

## 搭配不同

**comfort zone 高頻搭配**

- step out of / leave / get out of one's comfort zone（踏出舒適圈）
- push oneself out of one's comfort zone（逼自己離開舒適圈）
- stay in / be stuck in one's comfort zone（待在、困在舒適圈）
- outside one's comfort zone（在舒適圈之外）
- challenge oneself（挑戰自己）

**常見錯誤**

- 錯：step out comfort zone（少了 of 和所有格）
- 對：step out of my comfort zone
- 錯：go out of the comfort circle（舒適圈不是 circle）
- 對：go out of my comfort zone

## 翻譯與作文

**中文對應**

- 踏出舒適圈、走出舒適圈 → step out of one's comfort zone
- 挑戰自己 → challenge oneself
- 安於現狀 → be content with the status quo
- 嘗試新事物 → try new things
- 提醒：「舒適圈」不能直譯成 comfortable circle 或 comfort circle。

**翻譯示範 1**
想要進步，就必須勇敢踏出舒適圈。
→ To make progress, we must have the courage to step out of our comfort zone.

- 「想要進步」用 To make progress 放句首，表示目的。
- 「勇敢」拆成 have the courage to V，比 bravely 更像作文用語。
- 「踏出舒適圈」整塊用 step out of our comfort zone，of 後面接所有格加名詞。

**翻譯示範 2**
加入演講社讓我挑戰自己，走出了舒適圈。
→ Joining the speech club challenged me to step out of my comfort zone.

- 「加入演講社」當主詞，用動名詞 Joining。
- 「讓我挑戰自己，走出舒適圈」整合成 challenged me to V，不要拆成兩個動詞硬接。

**作文句型**

- Only by stepping out of our comfort zone can we ___.（議論說理、心得反思）
  - Only by stepping out of our comfort zone can we discover our true potential.
  - Only by 放句首時，後面的主詞和助動詞要倒裝，變成 can we。
- Instead of being satisfied with the status quo, I decided to ___.（經驗敘述、轉折）
  - Instead of being satisfied with the status quo, I decided to join the speech club and challenge myself.
- Stepping out of my comfort zone taught me that ___.（心得反思，結尾段）
  - Stepping out of my comfort zone taught me that failure is a necessary part of growth.

**升級替換**

- try something new → step out of one's comfort zone
- do something difficult → challenge oneself
- 如果只是描述「去上一堂新的課」這種小事，不一定要用 comfort zone。這個說法適合有「克服不安、突破自己」的情境，沒有挑戰感的事情硬用會顯得誇大。

**中式英文陷阱**

- 錯：I stepped my comfort zone and joined the club. → 對：I stepped out of my comfort zone and joined the club.
- 錯：We should not stay in the status quo. → 對：We should not be satisfied with the status quo. / We should not stay in our comfort zone.
- 錯：The comfort zone of mine made me afraid to try. → 對：My comfort zone kept me from trying new things.
