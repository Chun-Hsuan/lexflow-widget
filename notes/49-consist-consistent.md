---
title: consist 與 consistent
type: 辨析
core: consist of 是「由……組成」，be consistent with 是「與……一致」；consist in 少用，改用 lie in。
---

## 結論

大部分好用，但要挑著用：

- **consist of**：很好用，說明「由什麼組成」時直接套。
- **be consistent with**：很好用，議論文、圖表描述都能用。
- **be consistent in**、形容詞 **consistent**：好用，適合談態度、努力、表現的穩定。
- **consist in**：不建議在作文用。意思是「在於」，但日常寫作更自然的是 lie in，而且 consist in 和 consist of 只差一個介詞，寫錯的風險高。

| 用法 | 作文好用嗎 | 用在哪裡 |
|---|---|---|
| consist of | 好用 | 介紹團體、組織、活動、事物的組成 |
| be consistent with | 好用 | 圖表描述、舉證、說明言行是否一致 |
| be consistent in | 好用 | 談態度、習慣、努力是否穩定 |
| consist in | 少用 | 改用 lie in |

## 用法不同

**consist of：不及物，沒有被動，不能用進行式**

- 對：The club consists of twenty students from different grades.（這個社團由二十名來自不同年級的學生組成。）
- 錯：The club is consisted of twenty students.
- 錯：The club is consisting of twenty students.
- 可以改成分詞，放在名詞後面修飾：a club consisting of twenty students.

**consist of 和 include 不能直接互換**

- consist of 列出的是全部成分，include 可以只列一部分。
- 對：The price includes tax.（價格含稅，但還有其他項目。）
- 錯：The price consists of tax.
- 對：The team consists of five members.（成員就是這五位。）

**consist of 和 be made up of / be composed of：可以混用**

- The team consists of five members. = The team is made up of five members. = The team is composed of five members.
- 寫作時任選一個，consist of 最短。注意 be made up of 和 be composed of 本身是被動形式，只有 consist of 沒有被動。（更多組成相關的說法見 constitute / comprise 一則。）

**consist in：後面接名詞或 V-ing，意思是「在於」**

- 對：Happiness consists in being content with what we have.（快樂在於知足。）
- 同樣的句子用 lie in 更自然：Happiness lies in being content with what we have.

**be consistent with / in：介系詞決定意思**

- be consistent with + N：與……一致、沒有矛盾
  - 對：The results are consistent with our expectations.（結果與我們的預期一致。）
- be consistent in + N / V-ing：對……始終如一
  - 對：She is consistent in her attitude toward work.（她對工作的態度始終如一。）
- 錯：be consistent to
- 錯：The results are consist with our expectations.（詞性錯，要用形容詞 consistent）

**consistent 當形容詞，可以直接修飾名詞**

- consistent effort（持續的努力）、consistent performance（穩定的表現）
- 反義：inconsistent（不一致的）
- 相關：consistency（n. 一致性）、consistently（adv. 始終如一地）

## 高頻搭配

- consist of + N（由……組成）
- a group / team consisting of ___（由……組成的團體）
- be consistent with the facts / the data / one's words（與事實、數據、言論一致）
- consistent effort / quality / performance（持續的努力、穩定的品質與表現）
- be consistent in one's attitude / behavior（態度、行為始終如一）
- consistently perform well（一直表現良好）

## 翻譯與作文

**中文對應**

- 由……組成、由……構成 → consist of
- 與……一致、不矛盾 → be consistent with
- 對……始終如一、一貫 → be consistent in
- 持續的努力、穩定的表現 → consistent effort / performance
- 成功的關鍵「在於」…… → The key to success lies in ___（不建議 consist in）
- 提醒：「包括」不是 consist of，要用 include。

**翻譯示範 1**
這個社團由二十名來自不同年級的學生組成。
→ The club consists of twenty students from different grades.

- 「由……組成」對應 consists of，主詞 club 是單數，所以動詞加 s。
- 不要翻成 is consisted of，這個字沒有被動。

**翻譯示範 2**
他說的話與事實不一致。
→ What he said is not consistent with the facts.

- 「與……一致」對應 be consistent with，加上 not 變成「不一致」。
- 「他說的話」用 What he said 當主詞，也可以寫 His words are not consistent with the facts.

**翻譯示範 3**
成功的關鍵在於持續的努力。
→ The key to success lies in consistent effort.

- 「在於」用 lies in，主詞 the key 是單數，所以是 lies。
- 「持續的努力」對應 consistent effort，一次用上 consistent 的形容詞用法。

**作文句型**

- ___ consists of ___.（介紹組織、活動、課程）
  - Our school's music festival consists of three parts: a talent show, a food fair, and a concert.
- Our words should be consistent with our actions.（議論說理，談誠信、責任）
  - Our words should be consistent with our actions, or no one will trust us.
- Being consistent in ___ is the key to ___.（心得反思、議論說理）
  - Being consistent in our daily study habits is the key to making progress.
- The results are consistent with ___.（圖表描述、引用調查）
  - The results are consistent with the survey conducted last year.

**升級替換**

- keep doing something every day → be consistent in doing something
- is made up of → consists of（寫作時更簡潔）
- 「說到做到」→ be consistent with one's words and actions（較正式，日常聊天不用）
- 提醒：consist of 後面是「全部成分」，只列出部分時請改用 include。

**中式英文陷阱**

- 錯：Our class is consisted of thirty students. → 對：Our class consists of thirty students.
- 錯：The key to success consists of hard work.（想表達「在於」）→ 對：The key to success lies in hard work.
- 錯：The price consists of tax and service fees.（想表達「包含」）→ 對：The price includes tax and service fees.
- 錯：His behavior is consistent to his words. → 對：His behavior is consistent with his words.
