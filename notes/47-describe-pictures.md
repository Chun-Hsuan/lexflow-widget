---
title: 作文描寫圖片的動詞
type: 辨析
core: 描寫內容用 show / depict，傳達訊息用 convey；describe 要用「人」當主詞，圖片當受詞。
---

## 結論

- 描寫圖片「裡面有什麼」：show 最通用，depict 也很好；illustrate 在這裡也通順，不算錯。
- 說明圖片「想表達什麼」：convey（傳達訊息）、illustrate（說明道理）都適合。
- describe 是「人」去描述圖片，**圖片要當受詞**，不能拿 illustrate 來取代它。
- 第一段的順序：先用 show / depict 描寫兩張圖的內容，最後一句再用 convey 帶出共同訊息，銜接第二段。

## 重點

### show

- 句型：show + 名詞 / show + 名詞 + V-ing / show + that 子句
- 對：The picture shows a crowded street.（圖中是一條擁擠的街道。）
- 對：The picture shows two children playing in the rain.（圖中兩個孩子正在雨中玩耍。）
- 對：The picture shows that people are getting busier.（圖片顯示人們越來越忙。）
- 錯：The picture shows a boy is reading.（兩個動詞擠在一起）
- 對：The picture shows a boy reading.
- 搭配：show a contrast between A and B

### depict

- 用在畫作、照片、電影、小說等「描繪人事物」的作品。
- 句型：depict + 名詞 / depict A as B（把 A 描繪成 B）
- 對：The painting depicts a quiet village at night.（這幅畫描繪夜晚寧靜的村莊。）
- 對：The movie depicts the teacher as a kind and patient person.
- 不自然：The chart depicts that sales increased.（不接 that 子句）
- 對：The chart shows that sales increased.
- 搭配：depict a scene of ___；be vividly depicted

### illustrate

- 核心是「用例子、圖表、圖像來說明某個道理」，後面最順的是抽象內容（how 子句、趨勢、論點）。
- 句型：illustrate + 道理 / illustrate how ___ / illustrate A with B
- 對：The graph illustrates how prices have risen over the years.（這張圖表說明價格多年來是如何上升的。）
- 對：To illustrate this point, take my own experience as an example.
- 描寫具體畫面也通順，不算錯：The picture illustrates a boy reading in the library.（重心是「說明」，要最保險時用 show / depict 描寫內容。）
- 另有「為書籍畫插圖」之義（an illustrated book、illustrator），學測少見。

### describe

- 及物動詞，圖片當**受詞**，主詞是寫作的人。
- 句型：describe + 受詞 / describe A as B / describe how / what ___ / describe A to B
- 對：In this essay, I will describe the two pictures first.（本文先描述這兩張圖。）
- 對：He described the movie as boring.（他形容那部電影很無聊。）
- 對：Please describe the scene to me.（請向我描述那個場景。）
- 錯：describe about the picture（及物動詞，不加 about）
- 對：describe the picture
- 錯：illustrate the two pictures（想說「描述兩張圖」）→ 對：describe the two pictures
- The picture describes ___（圖片當主詞）不算文法錯誤，但學測作文裡較少這樣寫，建議改用 shows / depicts，或改成「人」當主詞去 describe。

### convey（描寫完內容後，用來帶出訊息）

- 受詞是訊息、想法、感受，不是畫面。
- 句型：convey + message / idea / a sense of ___ / feeling of ___
- 對：The picture conveys the message that reading is a source of happiness.
- 錯：The first picture conveys a boy reading in the library.（描寫畫面要用 show）
- 主詞是圖片、文字、音樂，不是「我」。
- 錯：I convey that people should ___. → 對：The pictures convey the message that people should ___.
- 搭配：Together, the two pictures convey ___.；While the first conveys ___, the second conveys ___.

## 可以混用

- 描寫具體畫面：show / depict / illustrate 都通順，不要硬說誰錯。
- 傳達訊息：convey 和 express 大多可以混用（但「表達自己」只能用 express oneself）。
- 同一段不要連用三個 show，可以第一張 shows、第二張 depicts。

## 翻譯與作文

### 中文對應

- 圖中顯示、圖片呈現 → show
- 描繪……的場景、把……描繪成…… → depict / depict A as B
- 說明、舉例說明 → illustrate
- 描述這兩張圖 → describe the two pictures（圖片當受詞）
- 傳達出……的訊息 → convey the message that ___

### 句型模板

- The first picture shows / depicts ___ V-ing ___, whereas the second one ___.
  - The first picture depicts a girl studying alone at night, whereas the second one shows a group of students discussing a problem together.
- Together, the two pictures convey the message that ___.
  - Together, the two pictures convey the message that technology can either bring us closer or push us apart.
- While the first picture conveys a sense of ___, the second one conveys ___.
- To illustrate this point, take ___ as an example.（第二段舉例時用）

### 第一段示範

The two pictures present a sharp contrast in how people spend time together. In the first picture, a group of friends are sitting around a table, but everyone is staring at his or her smartphone without saying a word. In the second picture, a family is playing a board game and laughing happily. Together, the pictures convey the message that technology can either bring us closer or push us apart, depending on how we use it.

### 中式英文陷阱

- 錯：The picture showed ___（描寫圖片用過去式）→ 對：The picture shows ___（用現在式）
- 錯：The picture conveys a girl crying. → 對：The picture shows a girl crying.
- 錯：The picture convey us the message. → 對：The picture conveys the message that ___.（單數加 s，不直接接人）
- 錯：convey him my thanks → 對：convey my thanks to him
- 錯：The chart depicts that ___. → 對：The chart shows that ___.
