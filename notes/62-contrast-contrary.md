---
title: contrast vs contrary
type: 辨析
core: 兩件事比較用 contrast；違背看法或預期用 contrary；by contrast 比較，on the contrary 否定。
---

## 結論

- 中文都說「相反」，但要看情況：兩件事比較用 contrast，違背看法或預期才用 contrary。
- by contrast 是把兩件事放在一起比較，兩句都是真的；on the contrary 是否定前一句，說「不是你想的那樣」。
- in contrast to 比較兩個對象；contrary to 對的是某個看法、預期、事實。

## contrast

**n. 對比、差異**

- 句型：the contrast between A and B；a contrast to / with ...
- 例：There is a sharp contrast between the two brothers.（這對兄弟形成強烈對比。）

**vt. 對照、對比**

- 句型：contrast A with B（將 A 與 B 對照）
- 例：The teacher asked us to contrast city life with country life.（老師要我們比較城市生活與鄉村生活。）

**vi. 與……形成對比**

- 句型：A contrasts with B；A contrasts sharply with B
- 例：His calm voice contrasted with his angry words.（他平靜的聲音和憤怒的言詞形成對比。）

名詞重音在前（CON-trast），動詞重音在後（con-TRAST），聽力會遇到。

**搭配**

- a sharp / striking contrast：強烈的對比
- in sharp contrast to ...：與……形成強烈對比
- by contrast / in contrast：相對之下（放句首，後面加逗號）
- in contrast to / with + 名詞：與……相比
- form a contrast with ...：與……形成對比
- contrast sharply with ...：與……截然不同

## contrary

**a. 相反的、對立的**

- 例：They hold contrary opinions about the plan.（他們對這個計畫的看法相反。）

**片語中的 contrary**

- be contrary to ...：與……相反、違反。例：His actions are contrary to what he says.（他的行為和他說的話相反。）
- Contrary to ..., S + V：與……相反（常放句首）。例：Contrary to popular belief, bats are not blind.（與一般人的想法相反，蝙蝠並不是瞎的。）
- on the contrary：恰恰相反（否定前一句話）。
- to the contrary：相反的。例：evidence to the contrary（相反的證據）。

contrary 另有「固執的」意思，學測少見。

## 容易選錯的地方

**by contrast 和 on the contrary：使用範圍不同**

- by contrast：把兩件事放在一起比較，兩句都是真的。
- on the contrary：否定前一句，說「不是你想的那樣」。
- 對：Taipei is humid. By contrast, Taichung is dry.（台北很潮濕，相對之下台中很乾燥。）
- 錯：Taipei is humid. On the contrary, Taichung is dry.（兩句只是比較，沒有人否定誰。）
- 對：Are you tired? On the contrary, I feel full of energy.（你累了嗎？恰恰相反，我精神很好。）
- 錯：Are you tired? By contrast, I feel full of energy.

**in contrast to 和 contrary to：使用範圍不同**

- in contrast to：兩個對象比較。
- contrary to：與某個看法、預期、事實相違背。
- 對：In contrast to his brother, Tom is quiet.（和哥哥相比，湯姆很安靜。）
- 錯：Contrary to his brother, Tom is quiet.
- 對：Contrary to our expectations, the test was easy.（與我們的預期相反，考試很簡單。）
- 錯：In contrast to our expectations, the test was easy.

**詞性與介系詞：用法不同**

- contrast 不是形容詞，形容詞是 contrasting（對比的、截然不同的）。例：contrasting colors。
- contrary 後面接 to，不接 with；contrast 當動詞時才接 with。

## 詞性變化與同字根字

- contrasting a.：對比的、截然不同的
- contrary 和 contrast 的字首 contra- 都是「相對、相反」，學測範圍還有 contradict（反駁、與……矛盾）、contradiction（矛盾）、controversy（爭議）。

## 翻譯與作文

**中文對應**

- 與……形成對比、相比之下 → in contrast to / by contrast
- 兩者形成強烈對比 → be in sharp contrast
- 與一般想法相反、出乎預期 → contrary to popular belief / contrary to expectations
- 恰恰相反 → on the contrary

**翻譯示範 1**

- 中文：與一般人的想法相反，金錢並不總是帶來快樂。
- 英文：Contrary to what most people believe, money does not always bring happiness.
- 「與一般人的想法相反」對應 Contrary to + 名詞或 what 子句。「想法」是被違背的對象，所以用 contrary to，不用 in contrast to。

**翻譯示範 2**

- 中文：相較於哥哥的外向，弟弟安靜多了。
- 英文：In contrast to his outgoing brother, the younger boy is much quieter.
- 哥哥和弟弟是兩個比較對象，所以用 in contrast to。這裡最容易誤用 contrary to。

**作文句型**

- 圖表描述、比較兩件事：___. By contrast, ___.
  - Sales of paper books kept falling. By contrast, e-book sales kept rising.
- 描寫對比的畫面（敘事、心得）：___ is in sharp contrast to ___.
  - The quiet countryside is in sharp contrast to the noisy city.
- 議論開頭，推翻常見看法：Contrary to popular belief, ___.
  - Contrary to popular belief, failure is not always a bad thing.
- 反駁前一句的看法：Some people think ___. On the contrary, ___.
  - Some people think failure is a waste of time. On the contrary, it teaches us valuable lessons.

**升級替換**

- different from / unlike → in contrast to，例如 Unlike my sister, I enjoy sports. 可以升級成 In contrast to my sister, I enjoy sports.
- however / but → by contrast，只有在前後兩句是並列比較時才換，轉折用 however 就好。
- a big difference → a striking / sharp contrast。
- 句子簡單時用 unlike 反而更自然，不用硬換。

**中式英文陷阱**

- 錯：In contrary, I think it is not true.
- 對：On the contrary, I think it is not true.（on the contrary 要加 the，也不能寫成 in contrary）
- 錯：The two cities are very contrast.
- 對：The two cities are in sharp contrast. / The two cities contrast sharply.
- 錯：Contrary to my sister, I like sports.
- 對：In contrast to my sister, I like sports. / Unlike my sister, I like sports.
- 錯：His words are contrary with his actions.
- 對：His words are contrary to his actions.
