---

## 結論

- 作文表達立場，不必一直寫 I think。基礎說法適合一般寫作，更有力的說法適合議論文。
- 常見的結構是先承認反方觀點（讓步），再用有力的說法帶出自己的立場。

## 基礎實用型

直接、明瞭，通常以第一人稱（I、my）開頭。

- **In my opinion, ...**（依我之見，……）
  - In my opinion, layout A is more user-friendly.
- **From my perspective, ... / From my point of view, ...**（從我的角度來看，……）
  - From my perspective, the long-term benefits outweigh the initial costs.
- **I believe / maintain / contend that ...**（我相信／主張／堅稱……）
  - I contend that technology should supplement, not replace, teachers.

## 立場更有力的說法

在議論文中，過度使用 I think 會顯得單調。下面幾個結構既帶出立場，也顯得成熟：

- **It is my belief that ...**（我深信……）
  - It is my belief that strict laws can reduce crime rates.
- **I am convinced that ...**（我確信……）
  - I am convinced that renewable energy is the only way forward.
- **I am of the opinion that ...**（我持有……的看法，較正式）
  - I am of the opinion that physical books will never completely disappear.
- **I strongly support the idea that ...**（我強烈支持……的觀點）
  - I strongly support the idea that education should be free for all.

## 如何在文章中切入立場

先承認反方觀點，再用有力的說法轉折：

- While some people argue that social media connects the world, I am convinced that it actually increases social isolation.（雖然有些人認為社群媒體連結了世界，但我深信它實際上增加了社交孤立。）

## 可以混用

- in my opinion 和 from my point of view 在作文中可以互換。*
- I believe that ... 和 I am convinced that ... 都通順，後者語氣更堅定。

## 中式英文陷阱

- 錯：According to me, schools should ban phones.
- 對：In my opinion, schools should ban phones.*
- 錯：In my point of view, ...
- 對：From my point of view, ...*

## 翻譯與作文

- 中文對應：「依我之見」→ in my opinion；「從我的角度來看」→ from my perspective；「我深信」→ I am convinced that；「雖然……但我認為……」→ While ..., I believe that ...。
- 作文句型：While some people argue that ___, I am convinced that ___.
  - While some people argue that homework is useless, I am convinced that it helps students build good habits.*
