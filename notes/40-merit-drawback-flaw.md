---
title: merit / drawback / flaw
type: 辨析
core: 談事物優缺點用 advantages and disadvantages 最保險；flaw 是缺陷，談人才用 flaw / fault / weakness。
---

## 結論

- 談「某件事的優缺點」時，merits and demerits、pros and cons、advantages and disadvantages、good points and bad points 都通順，可以混用；寫作最保險的是 advantages and disadvantages。
- flaw 是「缺陷、瑕疵」，不是通用的「缺點」；談事物的壞處要用 disadvantage / drawback，談人的缺點才用 flaw / fault / weakness。
- drawback 是寫作中最好用的「缺點」，談事物的壞處時和 disadvantage 可以互換。
- merit 本身很好用；demerit 學測少見，寫作建議改用 drawback / disadvantage。
- flawness 不是單字。名詞 flaw，形容詞 flawed（有缺陷的）、flawless（完美無瑕的）。
- 這幾個字當「缺點」用時全部是可數名詞，單數要加 a，不能光禿禿地寫。

## merit

- 搭配：the merits of ___、have its merits、weigh the merits of ___
- 另一義：價值、長處（a book of great merit）
- 對：Online learning has its merits.（線上學習有它的優點。）
- 和 advantage 可以混用：the merits of / the advantages of online learning 都通順。

## demerit

- 多半和 merit 成對出現（merits and demerits）。
- 另一義：學校或駕駛的「記過」。He got three demerits for being late.
- 學測少見，寫作建議改用 drawback / disadvantage。
- 對：We should consider the merits and demerits of the plan.

## cons

- 幾乎只出現在固定搭配 pros and cons，順序固定。
- 錯：the cons and pros
- 對：weigh the pros and cons
- 單數 con 另有「騙局」的意思（a con man 是騙子），寫作不建議單獨用 a con。

## bad point

- 口語和談人的特質時自然（good points and bad points）。
- 寫作文建議改用 disadvantage / drawback。

## drawback

- 詞性：n. 缺點、不利之處（可數）
- 搭配：a major / serious / obvious drawback、the main drawback of ___ is that ___、a drawback of / to ___、have drawbacks
- 對：The main drawback of this plan is its high cost.（這個計畫的主要缺點是成本太高。）
- 錯：This method has many drawback. → 對：This method has many drawbacks.
- 和 disadvantage 可以混用：a drawback of / a disadvantage of online classes 都通順。
- 談人的個性缺點不用 drawback，改用 flaw。

## flaw

- 用法：事物、計畫、論點或人本身的缺陷，常接 in。
- 對：There is a serious flaw in your plan.（你的計畫有個嚴重的漏洞。）
- 不自然：One flaw of living in a big city is the noise.
- 對：One disadvantage of living in a big city is the noise.
- 錯：flawness、the flawness of the plan
- 對：a flaw in the plan / the plan is flawed

## 講「人」的缺點：flaw / fault / weakness

- flaw：人的缺點，最通用。His only flaw is that he is too stubborn.
- fault：個性、行為上的毛病。His only fault is that he talks too much.
- weakness：能力、不擅長的地方。His only weakness is that he is bad at math.
- 錯：Math is his only fault. → 對：Math is his only weakness.
- 不自然：A's only disadvantage is that he is too stubborn. → 對：A's only flaw is that he is too stubborn.

## 可數與不可數

| 字 | 當「優缺點」用時 | 其他義項 |
|---|---|---|
| merit | 可數：a merit / merits | 「價值、長處」不可數：a book of great merit |
| demerit | 可數：a demerit / demerits | 「記過」也可數：three demerits |
| cons | 複數（單數 a con 另有「騙局」義） | 幾乎只用在 pros and cons |
| bad point | 可數：a bad point / bad points | --- |
| drawback | 可數：a drawback / drawbacks | --- |
| flaw | 可數：a flaw / flaws | --- |
| fault | 可數：a fault / faults（個性上的毛病） | 「過錯、責任」不可數：It is not my fault. |
| weakness | 可數：a weakness / weaknesses（弱點） | 「虛弱、軟弱」不可數：show weakness |

- 可數名詞單數前面一定要有 a / the / 所有格，不能光禿禿地寫。
- 錯：This plan has flaw. → 對：This plan has a flaw. / This plan has flaws.
- 錯：It has obvious drawback. → 對：It has an obvious drawback. / It has obvious drawbacks.（obvious 開頭是母音，前面用 an）

## 可以混用

- 談「某件事的優缺點」：merits and demerits / pros and cons / advantages and disadvantages / good points and bad points。
- drawback 和 disadvantage：談事物的缺點時可以互換。
- merit 和 advantage：可以互換。
- 個性類缺點（固執、愛遲到、愛說話）：flaw 和 fault 可以互換，選 flaw 就好。

## 翻譯與作文

**選字指南**

| 中文情境 | 用哪個字 |
|---|---|
| 比較某事的「優缺點」 | advantages and disadvantages（最保險）/ merits and drawbacks / pros and cons |
| 單講「某個缺點」 | a disadvantage / a drawback |
| 「缺陷、瑕疵、漏洞」 | a flaw |
| 「人的缺點」 | a flaw / a weakness |
| 「人不擅長的地方」 | a weakness |

**中文對應**

- 「有它的優點」→ have its merits
- 「衡量優缺點」→ weigh the pros and cons
- 「唯一的缺點就是……」→ ___'s only flaw is that ___
- 「缺陷、漏洞」→ a flaw in ___

**翻譯示範**

- 做決定之前，我們應該仔細衡量這個計畫的優缺點。→ Before making a decision, we should carefully weigh the pros and cons of the plan.
- 雖然這個方法有它的優點，但也有明顯的缺點。→ Although this method has its merits, it also has obvious drawbacks.
- 這個設計的缺陷導致了意外。→ The flaw in the design led to the accident.
- A 唯一的缺點就是太固執。→ A's only flaw is that he is too stubborn.（或 A's only flaw is being too stubborn.）
  - 錯：A's only flaw is too stubborn.（is 後面要接 that 子句或 V-ing）

**句型模板**

- There are both merits and demerits to ___. There are both merits and demerits to studying abroad.
- ___ has its merits, but ___. Studying alone has its merits, but it can also feel lonely.
- The greatest merit of ___ is that ___. The greatest merit of reading is that it widens our horizons.
- ___ is not without its flaws. This plan sounds attractive, but it is not without its flaws.
- A major drawback of ___ is that ___. A major drawback of social media is that it takes up too much of our time.
- ___'s only flaw is that ___. My brother's only flaw is that he never admits his mistakes.
- If I had to name one flaw of ___, it would be ___. If I had to name one flaw of my best friend, it would be her habit of being late.

**升級替換**

- good and bad points → merits and drawbacks / advantages and disadvantages
- bad point → drawback（談事物）、flaw（談人）
- advantages 和 merits 輪流使用，可以避免一篇文章中重複。
- 想緩和語氣：The only thing I would change about him is ___.
