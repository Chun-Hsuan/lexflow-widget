---
title: 感謝的五種說法
type: 辨析
core: thank 和 be grateful / thankful 接人；appreciate 接事物；appreciative 要加 of。
---

## 結論

- appreciate 是動詞，不能寫成 be appreciate to sb for sth。
- 表示「感謝某人某事」，有五種正確說法：thank、appreciate、grateful、thankful、appreciative。
- 判斷重點是後面接人還是接事物：thank、grateful、thankful 可以接人；appreciate、appreciative 接事物。

## 五種說法

| 說法 | 句型 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| thank（動詞） | thank sb for sth | Thank you for your help.（謝謝你的幫忙。） |
| appreciate（動詞） | appreciate + 事物 / V-ing / that 子句 | I appreciate your help.（我很感激你的幫助。） |
| grateful（形容詞） | be grateful to sb for sth | I am grateful to you for your support.（我很感激你對我的支持。） |
| thankful（形容詞） | be thankful to sb for sth | She is thankful to him for the ride.（她很感激他順路載她一程。） |
| appreciative（形容詞） | be appreciative of sth | We are appreciative of your hard work.（我們很感激你的辛勞。） |

## 用法不同

**appreciate：表達感謝時，後面接事物，不接人**

- 對：I appreciate your help.
- 對：I appreciate you helping me.（我很感激你幫我。）
- 錯：I appreciate you for your help.*
- 想把人放在後面，改用 thank sb for sth 或 be grateful to sb for sth。

**appreciate 是動詞，不能加 be**

- 錯：I am appreciate to you for your help.
- 對：I appreciate your help.

**appreciative：介系詞要用 of**

- 錯：We are appreciative to you for your hard work.
- 對：We are appreciative of your hard work.

## 翻譯與作文

- 中文對應：「謝謝你……」→ Thank you for ___；「我很感激你的……」→ I appreciate your ___ / I am grateful to you for ___。
- 翻譯示範：我很感激你在考試期間給我的支持。
  I am grateful to you for your support during the exam period.*
  接人用 grateful to you for，不寫成 appreciate to you。
- 作文句型：I would like to express my gratitude to ___ for ___.*
  - I would like to express my gratitude to my teacher for her patient guidance.*
- 中式英文陷阱：
  - 錯：Be appreciate to sb for sth.
  - 對：Thank sb for sth. / Be grateful to sb for sth.
