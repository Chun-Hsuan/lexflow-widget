---
title: ensure（確保）
type: 字詞
core: ensure 接事情不接人，後面接名詞或 that 子句；不接 to V，也不加 of。
---

## 結論

ensure 是及物動詞，意思是「確保（某事發生或成立）」。受詞是事情，不是人。學測最需要掌握的是兩個句型：**ensure + N** 和 **ensure that + 子句**。

## 核心句型

| 句型 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| ensure + N | The law ensures equal rights for everyone.（這項法律確保人人享有平等的權利。） |
| ensure that + S + V | The school ensures that every student has a chance to speak.（學校確保每位學生都有發言的機會。） |
| to ensure (that) ___（表目的，放句首或句尾） | To ensure everyone's safety, the school has set new rules.（為了確保大家的安全，學校訂了新規定。） |
| help ensure + N / that 子句 | Regular exercise helps ensure good health.（規律運動有助於確保身體健康。） |
| 被動：N + be ensured | Safety must be ensured at all times.（安全必須隨時受到確保。） |

## 高頻搭配

- ensure safety / quality / fairness / success / accuracy（確保安全、品質、公平、成功、正確性）
- ensure the safety of ___（確保……的安全）
- ensure that ___（確保……）
- help ensure ___（有助於確保……）
- take measures to ensure ___（採取措施確保……）

## 不能混用的字

- **assure**：向某人保證，後面有人。I assure you that ___.
- **insure**：投保。He insured his car.
- **make sure**：口語版，意思和 ensure 相同。作文用 ensure 更正式，日常寫作兩者都可以。
- 商品、服務的「保證」是 guarantee，不是 ensure。

## 常見錯誤

- 錯：We must ensure to lock the door.（ensure 後面不接 to V）
- 對：We must ensure that the door is locked. / We must make sure to lock the door.
- 錯：The teacher ensured us that the test would be easy.（ensure 不接人）
- 對：The teacher assured us that the test would be easy.
- 錯：We must assure the safety of our children.
- 對：We must ensure the safety of our children.

## 翻譯與作文

**中文對應**

- 確保、務必使……、為了確保…… → ensure
- 有助於確保…… → help ensure
- 採取措施以確保…… → take measures to ensure
- 提醒：中文「保證」要先判斷，對某人說的話用 assure，讓事情成立用 ensure。

**翻譯示範 1**
政府應該制定法律，確保每個人都能受到公平對待。
→ The government should pass laws to ensure that everyone is treated fairly.

- 「制定法律」用 pass laws，「確保」對應 ensure that + 子句。
- 「受到公平對待」是被動，寫成 is treated fairly，不要寫 can be fair treated。

**翻譯示範 2**
規律運動有助於確保身體健康。
→ Regular exercise helps ensure good health.

- help 後面可以直接接原形動詞，所以是 helps ensure，不用加 to。
- 「身體健康」用 good health，不需要寫成 body health。

**作文句型**

- To ensure that ___, ___ should ___.（議論說理，提出解決辦法）
  - To ensure that students stay healthy, schools should provide balanced lunches.
- It is important to ensure that ___.（議論說理，強調重點）
  - It is important to ensure that students get enough sleep.
- Measures should be taken to ensure ___.（議論說理，社會議題）
  - Measures should be taken to ensure the safety of children online.
- ___ helps ensure ___.（說明因果）
  - A balanced diet helps ensure that our bodies get the nutrients they need.

**升級替換**

- make sure that ___ → ensure that ___
- in order to make sure ___ → to ensure ___
- 這個字偏正式，適合議論文。寫日記或跟朋友聊天的語氣，用 make sure 更自然。

**中式英文陷阱**

- 錯：To ensure of the safety of children, we should install cameras.
- 對：To ensure the safety of children, we should install cameras.（ensure 後面不加 of）
- 不自然：Ensuring the safety is important.
- 對：Ensuring safety is important. / Ensuring the safety of students is important.（泛指的「安全」不加 the，要特指才加）
