---
title: imagine / envision / picture
type: 辨析
core: 想像畫面或未來時多半可混用；只有 imagine 能表示「猜想」；envision 用於願景；picture 一定要接受詞。
---

## 結論

- imagine、envision、visualize、picture 在「想像一個畫面、情境、未來」時大多可以混用。
- imagine 範圍最廣：只有它能表示「猜想」（I imagine you're tired），也只有它能單獨使用（Just imagine!）或接 why（I can't imagine why he said that）。
- envision 專用於構想未來或願景；visualize 和 picture 的重點是在腦中「看見」畫面。

## imagine

- 句型：imagine + N / V-ing / (that) 子句 / wh-
- 搭配：can't imagine、hard to imagine、Just imagine!
- 當「猜想、認為」用：I imagine you're tired.
- 相關詞：imagination（想像力）、imaginary、imaginative

- 對：I can't imagine living without a phone.
- 對：I imagine you're tired after the long trip.
- 對：I can't imagine why he said that.
- 錯：I can't imagine to live without a phone.
- 錯：I envision / visualize / picture you're tired after the long trip.

## envision

- 句型：envision + N（a future / a world / a society）；envision + 人 + as + 身分；envision oneself V-ing；envision a ___ where ___
- 想像的是未來可能發生、希望實現的事。

- 對：The mayor envisions a city with no traffic jams.
- 對：We envision a society where everyone can receive an education.
- 不自然：I envisioned what my childhood home looked like.（回憶過去用 imagine）

## visualize

- 句型：visualize + N / oneself V-ing / how / what
- 重點是在腦中「看見」一個畫面，常用於場景、成功的情境。visualize why 不自然，要接原因疑問時用 imagine。*
- 相關詞：visualization；data visualization（資料視覺化）

- 對：Try to visualize yourself giving the speech.
- 對：Try to visualize the scene described in the story.
- 不自然：The government visualized a plan to reduce pollution.（構想計畫用 envision）

## picture（動詞）

- 句型：picture + sb / sth（一定要接受詞，不能單獨使用）；picture oneself V-ing；picture sb as + 身分；Picture this: ...（開場用）
- 名詞 picture：圖片、照片；the big picture（整體情況）

- 對：I can't picture him as a teacher.
- 對：Picture yourself standing on a quiet beach.
- 錯：Just picture!（要說 Just picture it!）
- 錯：I can't picture myself to live abroad.
- 錯：I picture you're tired.

## 可以混用

- 想像一個未來或理想的情境：imagine / envision / visualize 都通順（She envisioned a world without war.）；picture 也可以，但較口語。
- 在腦中看見一個場景或自己的樣子：imagine / visualize / picture 都通順（Visualize yourself standing on the stage.）。
- 作文寫願景時選 envision；寫開頭、拉出畫面時選 picture。

## 翻譯與作文

- 中文對應：
  - 「想像、無法想像、想不通」→ imagine
  - 「我想你……（推測）」→ I imagine ...
  - 「構想未來、抱持願景」→ envision
  - 「在腦中浮現畫面、預先想像成功的樣子」→ visualize
  - 「想像他／我自己……的樣子」→ picture him / myself ...
- 翻譯示範：
  - 我無法想像沒有手機的生活。→ I can't imagine living without a phone.
  - 我們希望建立一個人人都能受教育的社會。→ We envision a society where everyone can receive an education.
  - 我無法想像他當老師的樣子。→ I can't picture him as a teacher.
- 句型模板：
  - I envision a ___ where ___.（I envision a school where students can express their ideas freely.）
  - It is hard to imagine ___ without ___.（It is hard to imagine modern life without the Internet.）
  - Before ___, try to visualize ___.（Before giving a speech, try to visualize yourself speaking confidently.）
  - Picture yourself ___.（Picture yourself standing on a quiet beach with the sun setting behind you.）
- 升級替換：
  - think of the future → envision a future
  - picture it in my mind → visualize it
  - 不要每個「想像」都用 envision；簡單的推測、疑問、驚嘆用 imagine 才自然。
  - picture 偏口語，議論文談理想與願景時改用 envision 比較穩。
- 中式英文陷阱：
  - 錯：imagine to live → 對：imagine living
  - 「想像力」是 imagination，不是 imagine power 或 picture power。
  - 「我想像你很累」是推測，只能用 imagine，不能用 envision / visualize / picture。
